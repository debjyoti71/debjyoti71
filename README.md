## About

I'm **Debjyoti Ghosh**, a Computer Science and Information Technology student at **UEM Kolkata**, graduating in 2027. I work across **AI/ML, cybersecurity, applied research, and backend engineering**—with a focus on turning ideas into useful, maintainable software.

- **AI/ML:** deep learning, NLP, computer vision, forecasting, and LLM applications
- **Cybersecurity:** threat-intelligence workflows, multi-tenant systems, and access control
- **Research:** multimodal deepfake detection, voice biometrics, and context-aware NLP
- **Backend:** APIs, data modeling, WebSockets, and asynchronous processing
- **Learning:** retrieval-augmented generation, agentic systems, and trustworthy AI

Completed **Amazon ML Summer School 2025**. Recognized for multimodal deepfake detection research at **IEEE INDICON 2025**.
